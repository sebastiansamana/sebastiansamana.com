Heat Detective ↗A Group 1 project · Manchester · 2026

A clearer path from
building to decision.

Heat Detective is a web-based concept by Group 1 for exploring how existing buildings can adapt to warmer summers without losing sight of winter comfort.

The proposed workflow

You bring a building model and explain what matters. The tool organises the evidence, maps constraints, asks useful questions, and helps compare ideas with sourced precedents and visible trade-offs.

What works on this page

The eight-feature tour, seasonal illustration views, help and downloadable brief are interactive. The building is an illustration. Model upload, AI suggestions, project storage and thermal simulation are not connected yet.

What needs evidence

Geometry alone cannot tell us what is structurally safe to change. Construction constraints need verification. Climate scenarios need separate simulation runs, and reversibility needs a project-specific review.
